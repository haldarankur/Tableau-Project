# Tableau-Project

Here I have created a collection of Tableau visualizations and dashboards in order to explore data, identify trends, uncover insights and present meaningful insights in a simple and interactive way with the assistance of data-driven decision-making.

## Project Objective
The Bike Haven is a sales analysis and business intelligence project focused on transforming the company's traditional, static sales reports into interactive dashboards. The project aims to provide meaningful insights into sales performance across products, customers, and time, enabling better tracking of business growth, comparison of actual sales against the 2023 budget, and data-driven decision-making through interactive visualizations and filters.

## Datasets used
-  <a href="https://github.com/haldarankur/Tableau-Project/blob/main/SalesBudget.csv">The Bike Haven Sales Budget</a>
-  <a href="https://github.com/haldarankur/Tableau-Project/blob/main/Tableau_FinalProject.tar">Dataset</a>

## Questions (KPIs)
-  Which are the Top 10 Products by Sales Amount?
-  Who are the Top 10 Customers based on sales?
-  How do Actual Sales Figures compare against Budgeted Sales Figures for each month?
-  What are the Sales Figures by Region/City?
-  How do Product Categories compare in terms of Sales Amount?
-  How do Sales Figures compare with the Previous Year?
-  Which Months recorded the highest sales?


-  Dashboard Interaction <a href="https://github.com/haldarankur/Tableau-Project/blob/main/dashboard.jpg">View Dashboard</a>

## Process
-  Restore the provided database in PostgreSQL using pgAdmin.
-  Clean, preprocess, and merge the relevant tables using SQL JOINs.
-  Export the processed tables into CSV format.
-  Import the CSV files and budget dataset into Tableau Desktop.
-  Create the required sales visualizations and year-over-year comparisons.
-  Develop an interactive dashboard with customer and product filters.
-  Format the dashboard in A4 portrait layout and export it as a PDF.

## Project Insight
-  Mountain-200, Black, 46 emerged as the highest-selling individual product.
-  Jordan Turner was the highest-spending customer among the top 10 customers.
-  London recorded the highest sales among the listed cities, followed by Paris and Wollongong.
-  Bikes generated the highest sales revenue among all product categories.
-  Female customers contributed slightly more to overall sales than male customers.
-  Midi-Couleurs recorded the highest sales among the listed regions in the address-level analysis.


## Final Conclusion
The Bike Haven sales analysis project provides a comprehensive overview of the company's sales performance through interactive visualizations and data-driven insights. By integrating multiple datasets using PostgreSQL and transforming the processed data into Tableau dashboards, the project highlights key findings across products, customers, geographical locations, and product categories. The analysis identifies the major revenue contributors and helps uncover important sales patterns, enabling a better understanding of the company's overall business performance.
